A design studio built for iGaming

WaySpin designs marketing creatives, brand identities and websites for online casinos, sportsbooks, affiliates and the agencies that serve them. iGaming is the only industry we work in.

One contact, a specialised team

You brief one person and get one reply. Behind that contact is a team of designers who work only on iGaming projects. We match each task to the designer who knows the format best, manage the deadline and review every file before it reaches you.

We already know the category

Bonus mechanics, VIP programmes, GEO differences and the limits on what a promo is allowed to say. We design with these in mind from the first draft, so your team spends its time on decisions instead of corrections.

Markets and languages

We work with brands in Europe, LATAM and Asia and deliver localised versions for each GEO in English, Ukrainian and other languages on request.
